Phase 2A was completed using an ImageNet-pretrained MaxViT-Tiny 384 model and a validation-selected hybrid ensemble strategy. The standalone MaxViT-Tiny model achieved high recall but produced substantially more false positives than the Phase 1 ResNet ensemble. The best validation-selected hybrid ensemble assigned weights of 0.90 to the Phase 1 baseline ResNet-18, 0.05 to the weighted-augmentation ResNet-18, and 0.05 to the MaxViT-Tiny model, with a selected threshold of 0.0275. On the held-out test set, the hybrid ensemble maintained the same recall as the Phase 1 ensemble (0.9615) and the same number of false negatives (1), while reducing false positives from 86 to 53. This represents a false-positive reduction of 33 cases (38.37%). The PPV improved from 0.2252 to 0.3205, specificity improved from 0.8054 to 0.8801, AUROC improved from 0.9766 to 0.9888, and AUPRC improved from 0.9023 to 0.9154. Per-centre analysis also showed a reduction in false positives in both centres, indicating that the hybrid model improved precision and specificity without compromising sensitivity.
