Phase 2B evaluated the statistical reliability and per-centre robustness of the Phase 2A selected hybrid ensemble using stratified bootstrap confidence intervals. Overall bootstrap analysis showed that the hybrid model consistently improved precision and specificity while maintaining recall. The mean false-positive reduction was 32.57 cases with a 95% CI of [21.00, 45.00], while the PPV gain was 0.0954 with a 95% CI of [0.0565, 0.1417]. Specificity also improved by a mean of 0.0737. Per-centre bootstrap analysis confirmed that the improvement was not limited to a single centre. In center_1, the hybrid reduced false positives by a mean of 22.18 cases with a 95% CI of [13.00, 33.00], while in center_2 it reduced false positives by a mean of 11.04 cases with a 95% CI of [3.00, 19.00]. Recall and false negatives remained unchanged in both centres. These findings support that the Phase 2A hybrid ensemble provides a more reliable high-sensitivity screening model by reducing false positives without sacrificing neoplasia detection.
